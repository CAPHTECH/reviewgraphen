# Phase D: full ReviewGraphen detection measurement

The additive full arm completed 40/40 isolated live trials for the existing 20 positive/control fix pairs. All accepted processes exited zero; every raw response passed the closed candidate parser, manifest binding, and the five-obligation denominator.

Known-target detection was 1/20 for each of B1, G3-proxy, and full ReviewGraphen. B1 and G3-proxy detected `real-unit-02`; full detected `real-unit-15`. Full versus each existing arm had one discordant success in each direction (two-sided exact McNemar p=1.0). Each 5% rate has a 95% Wilson interval of 0.9%–23.6%; this floor-level single replicate did not distinguish the arms.

Full emitted 14 positive and 21 control findings. One positive finding matched the selected root. Blind adjudication covered the other 13 positive findings and all 21 controls: 9 `valid_novel_defect`, 20 `duplicate`, 1 `false_positive`, and 4 `insufficient`. These are model dispositions, not verified or human-accepted defects, and do not support a precision estimate.

The original two-arm `real_run_summary.v1` correctly remained frozen but cannot summarize a full-only inventory. `real_full_run_summary.v1` and `summarize-real-full-run` were added as an explicit additive contract; the original B1/G3 results were not rewritten.

The complete report, raw responses, replay records, private scores, blind batches, decisions, and three-arm comparison are in `benchmarks/m7-real-v1/results/full-reviewgraphen-replicate-1/`.
