# Amendment 001 — stop after graph-only and route to intelligent-use evaluation

This amendment was written after `compact-r1` and `graph-only-r1`, before any
`source-rich` or `full` request.

The compact result was complete and recognized.  The graph-only result was also
complete through its trailer nonce, but the model abandoned the explicit
two-call receipt task, performed 12 unrelated follow-up Bash calls, and timed
out.  Its exploration shows that semantic graph content activated an inferred
code-review task; the phase-1 contrast therefore does not isolate byte size
from semantic content or objective retention.

At the operator's direction, the benchmark stops rather than spending two more
requests on still larger one-shot payloads.  This is a deviation from the
preregistered four-cell phase 1 and is reported as such.  No payload-size
failure boundary is claimed.  The next experiment evaluates intelligent,
incremental ReviewGraphen use directly: selective expansion, external-state
tracking, convergence, grounding, and independently judged finding quality.
